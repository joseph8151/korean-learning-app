Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

KoreanGo is a Korean learning app. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it. It is written to describe what the app actually does, not what it might do one day.

The short version. If you use KoreanGo without an account, nothing you do leaves your phone. If you create an account, we store your email and your learning progress so it follows you to a new device. We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising trackers.

1. Using KoreanGo without an account

You can install the app and study without signing up. In that case your lessons, streak, saved words and progress are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes that data permanently — it cannot be recovered, because we never had a copy.

2. What we collect if you create an account

Account information

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • If you sign in with Google: the email address and name Google provides

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Learning profile

  • Your Korean level and, if taken, your placement test result
  • Why you are learning Korean (travel, work, K-POP and so on)
  • Your daily study goal
  • Country and native language, if you choose to enter them

Learning progress

  • Lessons started and completed, and quiz scores
  • Vocabulary you have practised, and which words you find difficult
  • Saved words and phrases
  • Study streak, daily study minutes and XP
  • Achievements earned

This exists so your progress survives a lost or replaced phone, and so the app can show you the right review words at the right time.

Purchases

If you subscribe to KoreanGo Premium, Google Play processes the payment. We never see your card details. We receive and store the purchase identifier Google gives us so we know your subscription is valid and when it expires.

AI conversation practice

If you use the AI Korean Partner, the messages you type in that conversation are sent to our server and then to an AI model provider to generate a reply. We do not use your conversations to train AI models. If your version of the app uses the built-in practice mode, the replies are pre-written and nothing is sent anywhere.

3. What we do not collect

  • No location. Location permissions are explicitly blocked in the app.
  • No contacts, photos, or files.
  • No audio ever leaves your phone. Speaking practice records you so you can play the attempt back — see section 3a. That recording is never uploaded.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party ad or analytics trackers.

3a. The microphone

Speaking practice asks for the microphone so you can record yourself saying a phrase and play it back against the native audio. This is the only feature that uses the microphone, and it only records while you are holding the button down.

  • Nothing is uploaded. The recording is written to your phone's cache and is never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no speech recognition and no pronunciation scoring, on the device or on a server.
  • It is deleted straight away. The clip is removed when you move to the next phrase, when you record again, and when you leave the screen. Nothing is kept between sessions.
  • You can say no. Decline the permission and the rest of the screen still works — you can listen and repeat out loud without recording. You can revoke it at any time in your phone's settings.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis is performance of a contract with you (providing the app you signed up for) and, for optional features, your consent.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with services required to run the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and server functions.
  • Google Play — payment processing and subscription status.
  • An AI model provider — only the messages you type in AI conversation practice.

6. Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled on your device by your phone's operating system. We do not send them from a server and we do not receive any data when one appears. You can turn them off in the app, or in your phone's settings.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete your profile and learning progress. Purchase records may be kept longer where tax or accounting law requires it.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See and change your profile in the app under Profile.
  • Erase everything on your device with Profile → Reset All Data.
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and all associated data, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it, or to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

KoreanGo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Our database enforces row-level security, so your records can only be read by your own account. Payment verification happens on our server rather than in the app, so a modified app cannot grant itself a subscription.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses for those transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

13. Contact

Questions, data requests or account deletion: support@koreango.app
